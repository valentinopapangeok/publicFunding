## Suggested Geo-K Positioning

For a serious archaeology/EO proposal, the best angle is not generic "cultural heritage digitisation"; it is operational intelligence from EO:

- detection of looting risk and illegal excavation signatures using multi-temporal optical/SAR imagery;
- landscape and vegetation anomaly detection for preventive archaeology;
- climate and disaster risk monitoring for cultural/natural heritage sites;
- integration of satellite, drone and field evidence into a decision-support layer for heritage managers or law-enforcement users;
- explainable AI workflows with provenance and uncertainty reporting.

The immediate next step is to identify one or two credible users: a heritage authority, archaeological mission, cultural heritage research group, law-enforcement/cultural-goods unit, municipality, or site manager. Without that user anchor, the Horizon topics will be difficult to enter except as a technical partner in someone else's consortium.

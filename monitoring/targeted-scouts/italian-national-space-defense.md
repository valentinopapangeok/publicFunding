# Italian National Space / Research / Aeronautics Scout

Snapshot date: 2026-09-01

Scope: quick public-source scan for ASI, CNR and Aeronautica Militare opportunities that might fit Geo-K capabilities in Earth Observation, geospatial analytics, UAV/drone operations, image processing, wildfire/water/agriculture/infrastructure monitoring, and space-adjacent data services.

## ASI

Source checked:

- ASI calls and contests: https://www.asi.it/concorsi_e_opportunita/concorsi/
- ASI online selections: https://selezioni.asi.it/home?lang=it
- ASI Space and Blue procurement page found in search: https://www.asi.it/bandi_e_concorsi/procedura-negoziata-per-laffidamento-dei-servizi-di-ricerca-e-sviluppo-relativi-a-iniziative-a-supporto-della-space-economy-e-nello-specifico-delliniziativa-space-and-blue/

Findings:

- No active ASI online-selection records were visible in the public e-recruitment page at scan time.
- The ASI public calls page showed 2026 calls with deadlines in March-July 2026, therefore already expired on 2026-09-01.
- The strongest thematic hit was the ASI "Space and Blue" R&D procurement. It fits space economy and downstream services, but the page states a deadline extension to 2026-06-05, so it is closed for new submission.

Monitor implication:

- ASI is worth keeping as a source, but current value is watchlist/early warning rather than immediate action.
- Best next automation target: ASI calls page plus ASI online selections. Filter for "Space Economy", "IRIDE", "Osservazione della Terra", "servizi geospaziali", "PNRR", "ricerca e sviluppo", "telerilevamento", "dati satellitari", "acquisizione", "manifestazione di interesse".

## CNR

Source checked:

- CNR online selections: https://selezionionline.cnr.it/
- CNR URP/search-visible records found via web search.

Findings:

- Search-visible CNR hits include EO and satellite-data research/recruitment items, including IMAA/IMIOT, IREA, ISMAR and IBE records.
- Relevant examples found were scholarships/research contracts/procurement around Earth Observation, high-resolution land surface temperature data, satellite oceanography, precision agriculture, SpaceItUp/ASI, SAR and water-content topics.
- The records inspected were expired as of 2026-09-01 or were personnel/research-position calls rather than direct SME funding.
- CNR may still matter as a partner-discovery and project-intelligence source, not only as a bid source.

Monitor implication:

- Track CNR as "partner/project intelligence" unless a real procurement or collaboration call appears.
- Best next automation target: CNR e-recruitment/search pages and URP records. Filter for "Osservazione della Terra", "telerilevamento", "dati satellitari", "SAR", "Copernicus", "agricoltura di precisione", "idrologia", "incendi", "SpaceItUp", "ASI", "IRIDE", "IMIOT", "IREA", "ISMAR", "IMAA".

## Aeronautica Militare

Source checked:

- Aeronautica Militare tenders/contracts page: https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/amministrazione-trasparente/bandi-di-gara-e-contratti/
- Aeronautica tender records found via web search.

Findings:

- One strong procurement hit: "ID 2024676 - 3 STORMO - fornitura di Strumentazione GPS, Lidar, UAV e UGV per Capacita SPMS per Aerofotogrammetria", inserted 2026-08-18. This is directly adjacent to UAV/LiDAR/aerophotogrammetry capability, but the public page snippet did not expose a submission deadline.
- One long-running access route: "Avviso pubblico di manifestazione di interesse a partecipare alle procedure di affidamento ex art. 50 ... per il biennio 2026-2027", deadline 2027-12-31. This may be a vendor-list / preliminary market-consultation route, not a funded R&D call.
- Other space/air-domain hits included SSA civil works, SATCOM for MQ-9A, SAIP support, and defence-air data-system support. These look more like defence procurement/supplier opportunities than grant funding.

Monitor implication:

- Aeronautica is worth adding as a procurement watch source with a separate lens from grants.
- Immediate follow-up candidate: inspect the 3 Stormo UAV/LiDAR/aerophotogrammetry tender documents and the 2026-2027 expression-of-interest attachments to determine eligibility, categories, and whether Geo-K should register/submit supplier interest.

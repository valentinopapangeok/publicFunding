# Geo-K Monitoring Profile

This file separates Geo-K expertise themes from funding sources. Funding sources are where the monitor looks; themes are how results are scored and filtered.

## Funding Sources

Funding sources are configured in `monitoring/funding-sources.json`.

Current and planned source families:

- ESA-star Publication
- EU Funding & Tenders Portal
- LIFE / CINEA
- ECMWF Copernicus procurement
- FAO / UNGM procurement opportunities

## Geo-K Themes

Core themes:

- Earth Observation
- satellite image processing pipelines
- operational EO products and services
- AI, machine learning and deep learning
- onboard AI, edge processing and autonomous systems
- drones, UAVs and image campaigns
- satellite mesh / communications-enabled EO operations
- SAR, SWIR and thermal data
- validation, veracity, provenance and data quality

Application themes:

- wildfire monitoring, burned area and burn severity
- water resources, hydrology, wetlands, drought, irrigation, flood, pollution, river and lake monitoring
- agriculture and crop monitoring
- archaeology, archaeological prospection and cultural heritage monitoring
- critical infrastructure monitoring, civil security, disaster resilience and emergency management

## Eligibility Filters

The monitor should keep:

- active opportunities only
- Italian, European, ESA-wide or otherwise international/wide eligibility
- single-company, SME-prime, subcontractor-light or user-supported opportunities
- calls where Geo-K can be prime contractor or credible specialist subcontractor

The monitor should discard or downgrade:

- country-specific calls outside Italy unless open to international suppliers
- opportunities requiring a large consortium as the normal bid structure
- manufacturing-heavy hardware calls
- launch, propulsion, components, mechanisms, avionics and flight-hardware opportunities
- calls where the only match is a generic word such as quality, satellite, thermal or processing

## Critical Infrastructure Interpretation

Critical infrastructure is relevant when the call is about monitoring, resilience, damage assessment, risk modelling, disaster impact, stress testing or geospatial intelligence for assets such as:

- transport corridors
- energy networks
- dams and water infrastructure
- ports
- industrial sites
- telecom infrastructure
- civil-protection assets

It is less relevant when the call is about cyber-only security, physical guards, access-control equipment, component manufacturing or policy work with no geospatial/EO angle.

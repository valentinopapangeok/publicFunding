# Funding Procedure Logbook

This logbook keeps reusable knowledge from funding/tender screening and proposal preparation. It deliberately avoids storing tender-specific bid work when a call is dropped.

## Dropped Calls

### ESA AO/1-13467/26/NL/IB/ov - ARCH-AI NWP/NOP EXPRO PLUS

Decision:

- Do not pursue because the available preparation time is too tight for a credible submission.

Useful knowledge retained:

- ESA-star tender/action ID was `1-13467 / 186387`.
- Relevant intended scope was Lot 1 - Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) only.
- ESA required standalone proposals per lot; an NWP proposal could not be technically, financially, or contractually conditional on the NOP lot.
- The Statement of Work required six representative ARCH-AI use cases.
- The work was a 24-month strategic study, not primarily a software build.
- Maximum ESA budget was EUR 500,000 per lot, Firm Fixed Price.
- A credible lean Geo-K-only internal team shape was:
  - one NWP / AI forecasting technical lead,
  - one EO architecture / data systems / verification technical lead,
  - one admin / project manager.
- A starting internal effort estimate was about 15-18 person-months, with 17 person-months as a reasonable baseline before ECOS costing.

Administrative lessons:

- Geo-K ESA Entity Code: `1000001558`.
- Geo-K Business Unit Code: `8000015143`.
- Geo-K VAT: `IT08983021000`.
- Geo-K SME status shown in esa-star: `No`.
- Geo-K LSI status shown in esa-star: `No`.
- For a Geo-K-only bid, the Consortium Structure page should show Geo-K as Prime Contractor, single phase, Firm Fixed Price, EUR.
- Complete the Declaration of Compliance only after the Cover Letter contains the relevant compliance declarations.
- The Key Acceptance Factors must match the proposal and cover eligibility, free competition, non-benefits, binding price, price type, budget cap and standalone-lot status.
- ECOS 6 is web-based in the ESA/esa-star environment; for current tenders, there is no local ECOS package to download.
- ECOS outputs normally needed for ESA tenders include PBF-1, PBF-2, PBF-3, milestone payment plan and any ECOS submission/export evidence required by esa-star.
- Uploading documents in esa-star is not enough. The proposal is submitted only when the dashboard is complete and the "Submit Proposal to ESA" action has been used.

### ESA 1-13525 / 187359 - Sustainable Water Resources and Wetland Ecosystems Management EXPRO+

Decision:

- Do not pursue because the available preparation time is too tight for a credible submission.

Useful knowledge retained:

- The tender concerned sustainable water resources and wetland ecosystems management.
- It appeared as an ESA-star EXPRO+ opportunity with multiple independent contract topics.
- The water-harvesting/drone idea for Sicily was explored only as an early fit check.
- The fit question centered on whether an EO-supported water-harvesting concept could be aligned to a water resources management tender rather than becoming a UAV hardware/deployment proposal.
- A private research center in Sicily, with links to local entities and Tunisia, could be relevant for future Mediterranean drought/water calls, especially if local decision-user access is required.

Administrative lessons:

- Clarification requests in esa-star are made through the tender's "ESA Clarification Requests" area.
- Clarifications should ask precise eligibility/scope questions, not pitch a project.
- The tender dashboard follows the same pattern as other ESA-star submissions: Declaration of Compliance, Key Acceptance Factors, Consortium Structure, proposal uploads, ECOS and CVs as applicable.

## General ESA-star Procedure Notes

- First verify the correct tender ID before working in the Bidder Restricted Area.
- Set up or confirm the Bidder Restricted Area for the exact tender and lot.
- Confirm whether the bid is Geo-K only or includes subcontractors before completing Consortium Structure or declarations.
- Keep the Cover Letter, esa-star declarations, KAFs, Consortium Structure and ECOS data consistent.
- CVs of Key Personnel are uploaded separately in esa-star.
- At least one Key Person should belong to the Prime Contractor where ESA asks for Key Personnel structure.
- WP managers should normally be listed as Key Personnel when the ESA template defines that rule.
- If AI systems are used to generate or substantially modify proposal elements or deliverables, disclose this where the ESA template and EXPRO conditions require it.
- Background IPR can include own software, datasets, methods, open-source software, commercial tools and third-party products if they affect deliverables.
- Treat live esa-star deadlines as authoritative when they differ from locally downloaded tender documents.
